Espresso & terracotta — applied to Endo AI

The Amperos palette and an editorial serif, shown on the real answer layout: trust chips, the boxed recommendation with its evidence-tier badge, author-style citations, and the tier-grouped bibliography with provenance badges. Two variants, because the reference is a marketing site (short text, big imagery) and this is a reading tool.

Palette

Deep espresso through warm taupe to cream, with a single terracotta accent carrying every action and citation. Sage green and amber are added for evidence-tier and warning states — the reference had no need for them.

Instrument Serif for headlines — the closest free face to the reference's high-contrast display serif — paired with Inter for body and data. The serif is what makes it read premium instead of generic-SaaS; used only at headline sizes, never for clinical prose.

Where the dark field earns its keep

Deep Learning's Final Verdict is short, declarative and re-read at the chair — exactly the kind of content the reference site's dark treatment suits. A hybrid is available: light for reading, espresso for the verdict and for the pitch deck.

The Final Verdict

Decision rule. If the tooth is a mature permanent molar with carious exposure and haemostasis is achieved within 5–10 minutes → full pulpotomy with a calcium-silicate cement, restored the same visit.
Anchored in: Level I network meta-regression of 21 RCTs (n=1,733) Gomez-Sosa E et al., J Dent. 2025
Does not apply when a periapical radiolucency is present → treat as irreversible and proceed to root canal treatment.
My read. Variant B is the one I'd ship for the app. The palette is what gives Amperos its character — the dark field is a marketing choice that works because the page is mostly photography and headlines. Endo AI asks a clinician to read several hundred words of evidence prose per answer, and long-form reading on espresso is measurably harder and fatiguing under clinic lighting. B keeps the warmth, the serif and the terracotta while staying readable.

Where A wins outright: the pitch deck, and the Final Verdict block above. Espresso plus that serif looks far more premium than the current plum-and-gold deck, and it would make product and pitch look like one company.
